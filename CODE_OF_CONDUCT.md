# Code of conduct

This is a small project that deals with money-related data, so respect and care matter.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to apps or to programming.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Protect privacy: never post real account numbers, phone numbers or names of other people.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or asking anyone to send money.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so. To report a problem, use the contact details on the [support page](https://gatogato999.github.io/beneficiaries/), or GitHub's "Report content" option.
