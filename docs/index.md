# My Beneficiaries · سجل المستفيدين

An independent helper app for Android: turn a bank or wallet account number into the 14-digit BBAN, and keep a private list of beneficiaries on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

*Not affiliated with any bank or wallet.*

## FAQ

**What is a BBAN and why do I need it?**
It is the full 14-digit account number some transfer systems require. The app adds the bank's prefix and suffix to your short account number.

**Some banks seem to have two numbers. Which one is used?**
The app builds the valid one automatically: leading zeros you type are handled for you.

**Can I move my beneficiaries to a new phone, or send them to a friend?**
Yes. Open the *Other* tab and use *Share* or *Save to device*, then open the file with the app on the other phone, or use *Import from file*. If the same person already exists, the app asks whether to merge or keep separate.

**Why does Android warn me when I install the APK?**
APK files installed outside Google Play show a standard warning. Install only from the link published by the developer.

**Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
No. Records stay on your device. Files you share leave the phone only because you chose to send them.

**Always check the receiver's name in your bank app before paying.**
